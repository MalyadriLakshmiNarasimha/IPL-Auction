## 2. END-TO-END SCENARIOS TO RUN

Use at least three independent sessions (two managers and one spectator), for example separate browser profiles or a Socket.IO test client. Record the result of each step.

1. Create an auction room; register and join managers.
2. Start and complete live bidding. Confirm every client sees the same player, bid and timer.
3. **Spectator security:** join as a spectator and attempt to bid, trade, submit a Playing XI and use host controls by sending raw Socket.IO events. All must be rejected by the server.
4. Verify purse and squad updates for every manager after each sale.
5. Complete the unsold auction.
6. Propose, reject, counter and complete player-for-player trades.
7. **Trade race conditions:** make simultaneous conflicting trade offers and acceptances involving the same player. Confirm no player is traded twice, no squad exceeds limits, and stale offers are invalidated.
8. **Trade rule enforcement:** try to send a cash or purse adjustment, trade a player the sender does not own, trade with yourself, and trade outside the trading stage. All must be rejected server-side.
9. **Restart test:** restart the server mid-auction and again after trading. Record what survives: room state, bids, pending trade offers, completed trades, drafted Playing XIs, rankings.
10. Calculate squad rankings from stored player statistics; independently recompute at least two teams by hand and compare.
11. Calculate manager rankings; confirm they differ from squad rankings where expected.
12. Draft, save and submit Playing XIs.
13. **Invalid Playing XI attempts:** 10 or 12 players, players not in the squad, duplicates, more than the overseas limit, no wicketkeeper, too few bowling options, submission after lock, and submitting another manager's XI. All must be rejected server-side with clear errors.
14. Calculate Playing XI rankings and confirm they are separate from squad rankings.
15. Generate and download each team's report (see section 5).
16. Verify the season recap numbers against the database, and all 7 awards against the stored scores.
17. Run the auction with the minimum and maximum number of managers, and with a manager who disconnects and reconnects during each stage.

## 4. DATABASE, PERSISTENCE AND SECURITY AUDIT

**Database**

- Inspect `prisma/schema.prisma`, all migrations, and every database operation for trades, Playing XIs, rankings, awards and reports.
- **In-memory fallback check:** inspect `src/lib/db.ts` and `server/db/index.ts`. Can production silently fall back to in-memory storage when MySQL is unreachable? That must be possible only when `NODE_ENV=test`. If production can fall back, mark it `FAILED` and explain what data would be lost on restart.
- Confirm which data lives only in server memory (live room state, bids, pending offers, XI drafts) and what is lost on a Render restart or sleep.
- Verify `prisma migrate deploy` would apply cleanly to a fresh hosted MySQL database, without destructive operations.
- Check transaction safety, unique constraints and duplicate-trade prevention.

**Security and authorization**

- List every Socket.IO event and REST endpoint, with its required role (admin, manager, spectator, anonymous) and where that role is enforced on the server.
- Verify authentication on the Socket.IO handshake and on every REST route.
- Check input validation on all payloads (types, ranges, IDs, string lengths).
- Check that one manager cannot act for another (bid, trade, respond, submit XI).
- Check rate limiting and error handling on sensitive events.
- Check for data leaks: spectators receiving purse or private manager data, other managers' draft Playing XIs visible before lock.
- Check CORS and cookie settings for the Netlify (frontend) to Render (backend) cross-domain setup: `SameSite`, `Secure`, allowed origins, and the `CLIENT_ORIGIN` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_SOCKET_URL` configuration.

**Configurability**

- Identify rules that are hardcoded (overseas limit, minimum bowlers, wicketkeeper minimum, squad size, trade limits, scoring weights). Recommend which should be room settings or versioned configuration.
